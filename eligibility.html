Is it right for me?Updated October 2, 2026

Eligibility

Ibogaine is not for everyone — and the exclusions are not fine print, they are the whole game.

Who tends to be a candidate

  • Opioid, alcohol, or stimulant dependence — especially where other approaches have repeatedly failed
  • PTSD, trauma, or treatment-resistant depression that has plateaued under conventional care
  • High-functioning but stuck — capable, outwardly fine, privately exhausted by a loop
  • Able to travel to a country where treatment is lawful, and serious about aftercare

Who is typically ruled out

  • Heart conditions — arrhythmia, prolonged QT, structural disease, prior cardiac events
  • Certain daily medications, including some antidepressants and methadone (transition protocols exist, but take time)
  • Severe liver or kidney impairment
  • History of psychosis · pregnancy

The exclusions exist because ibogaine affects the heart's electrical rhythm. If one applies to you, the conversation starts with a physician and an ECG, not a booking form — the full medical logic is on Safety & Protocols, and the alternatives page shows what still works if the answer is no.
